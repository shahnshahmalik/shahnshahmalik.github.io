02 Journey

From the first product to the current contract.

Four chapters. The rows are the story. The bars are the calendar — Appcarry starts in the last month of Estater and runs beside Prospecta, then Upwork. Choose a chapter to read it. Move across the years to see what overlapped.

2018 2020 2022 2024 2026 Now

Feb 2018 – May 2020

Estater

Senior front-end developer · Full-time · India

Property-analysis tools for Kuwait and Bahrain.

I built customer-facing tools for catchment analysis, price trends, and property benchmarking. Survey work was still living in documents and spreadsheets. I digitized those workflows, including CSV upload from legacy files, so field data landed in the product.

Where it started. Analysis tools someone else would use to make a decision.

Oct 2020 – Sep 2022

Prospecta Software

Senior front-end developer · Full-time · India

Data-governance screens on a platform fed by SAP.

I improved the UI across a suite of data integration, quality, and governance products handling large incoming datasets. The screens had to stay responsive and follow the way operators actually move through a 360° view of the data.

Where it widened. Enterprise scale, and interfaces for people who live in the data all day. Appcarry continued part-time beside this role.

May 2020 – Oct 2024

Appcarry

Front-end engineer · Part-time · India

Ad-tracking dashboards. Angular 12 to 17, and AI interface work along the way.

I built analytics dashboards and charted reports for impressions, events, conversions, and installs — filterable reports, dynamic forms, and a split into modules so the app stayed maintainable. Unit tests and performance work gave new features a stable base. From 2020 to 2022 this ran beside full-time work.

The long thread of dashboard craft. It starts as Estater ends, runs through Prospecta, and continues into the Upwork years.

Sep 2022 – present

Upwork

Front-end engineer · Freelance · Individual

Long-term Angular SaaS, contracted directly.

I bid as an individual, not through a firm. The contracts are long-running front-end work on Angular SaaS products. The client preference on record has been the United States and Canada.

The current chapter. Freelance, on contract, after the employed years.
