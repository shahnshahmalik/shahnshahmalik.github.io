02 Journey

Four chapters, from the first product to the work I do now. The dates overlap on purpose. Appcarry began as Estater was ending, ran alongside Prospecta, and continued into the Upwork years.

The scale runs from February 2018 to now. Move along the bars to see which roles overlapped in a given month.

Feb 2018 – May 2020

Estater

Senior front-end developer · Full-time · Based in India

I built property-analysis tools used in Kuwait and Bahrain.

Customers used them for catchment analysis of the surrounding area, for price trends, and for comparing properties. Survey results were still in documents and spreadsheets. I digitised those workflows, including CSV uploads from the old spreadsheet files, so the field data went into the product.

This is where the work started: tools that help someone else make a decision. The next role took that same kind of interface into much larger enterprise data.

Oct 2020 – Sep 2022

Prospecta Software

Senior front-end developer · Full-time · Based in India

I built data-governance screens on a platform that received its data from SAP.

The products covered bringing data in, checking its quality, and governing it, and they took in large datasets. I improved the screens so they stayed responsive, and so they followed the way the people using them actually move through a complete view of the data.

The scope widened here, to enterprise scale and to interfaces for people who spend the day in the data. Through this whole role, the Appcarry work continued part-time.

May 2020 – Oct 2024

Appcarry

Front-end engineer · Part-time · Based in India

I built ad-tracking dashboards, upgraded the app from Angular 12 to 17, and added recommendations and text completion to the interface.

The dashboards reported impressions, events, conversions, and installs. People could filter the reports, and the dynamic forms changed with the data. I split the application into modules so the parts stayed maintainable, and I kept tests and performance work in place so new features had a stable base. This was part-time from May 2020 to October 2024. Until 2022 it ran beside full-time employment. After that it ran beside the Upwork contracts.

This long run of dashboard work begins as Estater ends, continues through Prospecta, and overlaps my first independent years. I still build this kind of screen.

Sep 2022 – present

Upwork

Front-end engineer · Freelance · Independent

I now take long-running Angular contracts directly, as an individual.

I bid as myself, not through a firm. The contracts are front-end work on Angular products: floor plans you can interact with, analytics that change with a person’s role, and interfaces I take from a design through to the working product. I prefer clients in the United States and Canada.

This is the current chapter. The employed years are behind me, and the work is freelance, on contract.

03 Craft

What I do best, and what I love.

The list above is the range. Below is the work I most want to be hired for, and the work I want to keep doing.

01

From a picture of the product to the product.

Whether the design is a Figma file, a Photoshop file, a screenshot, or something looser, I build the Angular application. That means the components, the states, and the real data, until it behaves like the thing that was drawn.

02

Screens for people who work in the data.

Dashboards that depend on a person’s role, with charts, filters, dynamic forms, and floor plans. This is the governance suite at Prospecta and the tracking reports at Appcarry. The datasets are large, and the screens stay readable.

03

Faster screens, with tests.

I load only what the screen needs, and I fetch only the data it needs. Jasmine, Karma, Jest, and Cypress check the work before the next change.

What I love

Drawing on a plan

A floor plan is only a picture until you can add a zone, edit it, and style it. Changing the plan directly is the work I like most.

AI as part of the interface

I like building chat, text completion, and recommendations into a product. The model may be reached through an OpenAI-style API, or it may run locally through Ollama when the product should stay close to the user. The interface still has to be finished properly.

Finishing the interface

I care about the order of keyboard focus, the spacing, what the screen shows when it is empty or when something fails, and motion that shows what changed.
