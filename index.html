02 Journey

Four chapters, from the first product to the current contract. The calendar is the overlap — Appcarry starts as Estater ends, runs beside Prospecta, then beside Upwork.

Years from February 2018 to now. Hover the bars with a pointer to read which roles overlapped.

2018 2020 2022 2024 2026 Now
EstaterWhere it started 2018–20 ProspectaEnterprise data 2020–22 AppcarryDashboards, alongside 2020–24 UpworkFreelance, individual 2022–now

Feb 2018 – May 2020

Estater

Senior front-end developer · Full-time · India

Property-analysis tools for Kuwait and Bahrain.

I built customer-facing tools for catchment analysis, price trends, and property benchmarking. Survey work was still living in documents and spreadsheets. I digitized those workflows, including CSV upload from legacy files, so field data landed in the product.

Where it started. Analysis tools someone else would use to make a decision.

Oct 2020 – Sep 2022

Prospecta Software

Senior front-end developer · Full-time · India

Data-governance screens on a platform fed by SAP.

I improved the UI across a suite of data integration, quality, and governance products handling large incoming datasets. The screens had to stay responsive and follow the way operators actually move through a 360° view of the data.

Where it widened. Enterprise scale, and interfaces for people who live in the data all day. Appcarry continued part-time beside this role.

May 2020 – Oct 2024

Appcarry

Front-end engineer · Part-time · India

Ad-tracking dashboards. Angular 12 to 17, and AI interface work along the way.

I built analytics dashboards and charted reports for impressions, events, conversions, and installs — filterable reports, dynamic forms, and a split into modules so the app stayed maintainable. Unit tests and performance work gave new features a stable base. From 2020 to 2022 this ran beside full-time work.

The long thread of dashboard craft. It starts as Estater ends, runs through Prospecta, and continues into the Upwork years.

Sep 2022 – present

Upwork

Front-end engineer · Freelance · Individual

Long-term Angular SaaS, contracted directly.

I bid as an individual, not through a firm. The contracts are long-running front-end work on Angular SaaS products: interactive floor plans, role-based analytics, and interfaces taken from design through production. The client preference on record has been the United States and Canada.

The current chapter. Freelance, on contract, after the employed years.

03 Craft

What I do best, and what I love.

The skills above are the range. This is the part I am willing to be hired for, and the part I want to keep doing.

01

From a picture of the product to the product.

Figma, PSD, screenshot, or a looser design. I build the Angular application — components, states, and real data — until it behaves like the thing that was drawn.

02

Screens for people who operate the data.

Role-based dashboards, charts, filters, dynamic forms, and floor plans. Prospecta’s governance suite and Appcarry’s tracking reports. Large datasets, still legible.

03

Speed you can account for.

Lazy loading and tighter data fetching, with a test floor under the next change. Jasmine, Karma, Jest, and Cypress cover that work.

What I love

Drawing on a plan

A floor plan stops being a picture once you can add a zone, edit it, and style it. That kind of direct manipulation is the work I like most.

AI as part of the interface

Chat, text completion, recommendations. OpenAI-style APIs, and local models through Ollama when the product should run close to the user. The UI still has to be finished.

The last ten percent

Focus order, spacing, empty and error states, motion that shows what changed.
